# Talking to artists: why would they leave?

**Goal:** 5 real conversations. Not a survey. Ten minutes each, by call or voice note.

**Start with the 8 artists stuck on WhatsApp.** They are real people who started and stopped. What they say is worth more than any research.

## Rules
- Listen more than you talk. Do not sell Bonisa.
- Ask for a real story ("the last time"), not an opinion ("would you ever...").
- If she says something surprising, ask "tell me more about that".
- Write her words down exactly. Do not tidy them up.

## Opening (say this)
"Thank you for trying Bonisa. I am not selling anything. I want to understand what is hard so I can fix it. There are no wrong answers."

## Questions
1. **Today:** "Tell me about how a client booked you the last time. What happened, step by step?"
2. **Money:** "The last time a client did not show up or paid late, what did you do?"
3. **Stuck:** "When you got stuck on Bonisa, what were you trying to do? What did you expect to happen?"
4. **Leaving:** "What would make you stop using Bonisa and go back to WhatsApp and Instagram?"
5. **Worry:** "What worries you about getting bookings through an app?" (Listen for: fees, trust, getting paid, strangers, data.)
6. **Magic wand:** "If I could fix one thing this week, what should it be?"
7. **Close:** "Who is one other artist I should talk to?"

## Write down (one row per artist)
| Artist | Date | Where she got stuck | What she said about leaving (exact words) | What she wants fixed | Who she suggested |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
