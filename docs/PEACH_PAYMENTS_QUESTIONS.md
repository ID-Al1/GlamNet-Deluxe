Subject: Marketplace payments for a South African beauty platform (Bonisa)

Hi Peach team,

I run Bonisa, a South African platform where clients book verified beauty artists (makeup, nails, hair, braids, barbers). Bonisa is operated by Opus Intelligence (Pty) Ltd. We are getting ready to take our first live bookings and want to build our payments on Peach.

How it works: a client pays for a booking up front. We hold the money until the work is done and both sides confirm. Then the artist receives 82% and Bonisa keeps 18%.

Could you help with these questions?

1. **Holding money.** Can you hold a card or EFT payment and release it later, after the service is done? For how long?
2. **Splitting.** Can one payment be split between the artist and Bonisa (82/18), either when we release it or when it is paid?
3. **Paying artists.** How do artists get paid out? Do they each need to be set up with you, or can we pay them from our own account? How fast does a payout arrive?
4. **Refunds and disputes.** If a client is refunded or disputes a payment after we have paid the artist, who carries that cost?
5. **Payment methods.** We want cards and instant EFT for clients. Which banks are covered and what are the current fees for cards, Pay by Bank, Capitec Pay and Peach EFT?
6. **Gift bookings.** One person pays for a booking that another person will claim later. We plan to build this ourselves on top of a normal payment. Do you see any problem holding that money until it is claimed? Is there a gift voucher product we have missed?
7. **Onboarding.** What do you need from us to go live (documents, timelines), and is there a sandbox we can test in now?
8. **Our status.** We are pre-launch, with no live bookings yet. Is there a lower-volume pricing option, or a start-up programme?
