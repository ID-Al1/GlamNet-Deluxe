## New idea to review: Show Me Girl programme

Ambassadors sign in as clients but get their own title, level, credit balance, personal code and QR on their profile.

- She stays a client account with a Show Me Girl status on top, so we keep three roles and three dashboards.
- She gets her own badge, separate from the verified badge, so verification keeps its meaning.
- The owner switches the status on. Nobody can claim it.
- Credit is earned only when an artist she brought gets verified, or a client she brought completes a first paid booking. Signups and scans alone earn nothing.
- Credit comes out of Bonisa's 18%, so we start with points paid by hand until payments are settled.
