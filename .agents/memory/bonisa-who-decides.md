Alwande is the owner and makes the final call on money, rules and the non-negotiables. Haiden is the person working with Claude in these sessions and gives instructions, but a change to a non-negotiable (for example Stripe to Peach Payments) needs Alwande's confirmation.

**How to apply:** record session decisions as "instructed by Haiden, Alwande to confirm". Never write "confirmed by Alwande" unless Alwande himself said it.
