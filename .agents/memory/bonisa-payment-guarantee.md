Payment is a key moat. Three rules: (1) only Peach says whether money arrived, never screenshots or manual marking; (2) an artist never works on unsecured money, a booking shows "Deposit secured" only after Peach confirms; (3) money moves only by completion code, 24 hour complaint window, then payout with a visible pay-by date.

Cancellation is two tiers: 48 hours or more before is a full refund, less than 48 hours the artist keeps the deposit. No late fee (cannot be collected reliably after a service). Up to 30 minutes late the booking goes ahead and the artist may shorten the service; more than 30 minutes late is a no-show. The balance is paid through Bonisa by 24 hours before, otherwise the booking cancels and the artist keeps the deposit. Artist cancel or no-show is a full refund and a record against the artist.

**Why:** Alwande wanted simple rules and delegated the details on 2026-10-28. Full text in docs/BONISA_PAYMENTS_AND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md, numbers in lib/bookingPolicy.ts.
**How to apply:** do not add a late fee or a middle refund tier back without Alwande asking. Anything that moves money waits for Peach.
