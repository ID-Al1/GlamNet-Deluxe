THE STORY

THE LOOK

  • Neon purple and pink sunset over a pixel city
  • Pac-Man style maze walls along the street
  • Her phone copies the real Bonisa app: dark screen, dusty rose, an on the way banner, a next booking card and a bottom menu
  • Black outlines, shaded skin, soft ground shadows and motion trails, like classic pixel art
  • A pixel lotus blooms to reveal the diamond, sits in the salon window and holds the diamond at the end
  • Drawn on a tiny 320 by 180 grid, then blown up for the chunky pixels
