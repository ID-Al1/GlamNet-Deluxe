14. 82/18 PAYMENT SPLIT

This is a critical business rule.

Once BOTH:

1. Client confirms the service was completed
2. Artist confirms the service was completed

the system must automatically release the payment split.

Artist:

82%

Bonisa:

18%

Example:

Service price:
R 500

Artist receives:
R 410

Bonisa receives:
R 90

Do NOT release the artist’s 82% merely because the booking was created.

Do NOT release it merely because the client paid.

The completion confirmation logic must be enforced server-side.

The calculation must happen on the backend, not merely in the frontend.

The artist dashboard should show:

Net earnings

not simply gross booking value.
